2. Frequently Asked Inquiries

  • Does STASH download videos or MP3s?
    No. STASH streams media in-app directly from platform CDNs and embedded players with lock-screen audio support. It does not export or permanently store raw media files on your device.
  • How do I back up my library?
    Sign in with your Google account for automatic cross-device cloud synchronization, or export your portable vault archive via Settings → Export Vault (.json).
  • How do I delete my data or account?
    You can delete your cloud vault immediately in-app via Settings → Delete Account & Cloud Vault, or submit a request on our Account Deletion Page.
