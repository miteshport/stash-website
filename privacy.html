ARCHITECTURE & DATA PRIVACY

Privacy, by design.

Your library belongs on your device—not in an advertising database.

STASH is built around local-first storage, source transparency, on-device intelligence, and user-controlled data portability.

SPECIFICATION VERSION: 2.1 VERIFIED ARCHITECTURE: LOCAL-FIRST ZERO ADVERTISING TRACKERS
01 Storage Architecture

Your personal library stays local.

Most modern bookmarking tools copy your saves to central cloud databases to analyze your behavior, categorize your interests, and build audience segments. STASH is architected in reverse.

Your saved items, organized folders, recipe checklists, workout sets, and personal notes are stored directly within your device’s sandboxed local storage (storageService.ts). Downloaded media files write directly into local storage allocated to the app on your physical device.

If you use STASH without cloud backup, your vault data never leaves your physical phone. We operate no centralized database tracking what you read, cook, train, or save.

02 Data Brokerage

No ads. No behavioral tracking. No data brokerage.

STASH contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, zero social graph monitors, and zero programmatic advertising pixels.

Our application bundle contains only the core client runtime and native OS bridges. We do not sell, rent, license, or monetize your saved content, your search queries, or your reading habits to advertising networks, data brokers, or algorithmic aggregators.

03 Data Flow

What happens when you save.

Transparency means knowing exactly what data leaves your device, where it travels, and where processing takes place.

STEP 01 DEVICE

You Paste a Public Link

You share or paste a publicly accessible social URL (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X, Reddit). No personal credentials, session cookies, or account logins are transmitted.

STEP 02 NETWORK

Stateless Metadata Resolution

The URL queries public media stream resolvers solely to retrieve publicly available metadata: title, creator handle, caption text, and stream location. The query is stateless; no user identity is logged.

STEP 03 ON-DEVICE

Deterministic Intelligence

Content parsing runs 100% on your device (aiExtractor.ts). Ingredient parsing, unit conversion, and workout set extraction happen locally. Your notes are never sent to remote AI APIs.

STEP 04 LOCAL VAULT

Persisted to Your Storage

Structured cards and media are committed to your device's sandboxed storage. The item is indexed locally for instant, offline search.

ARCHITECTURAL TRUTH: At no point during saving does your device upload your personal library history, other saved cards, or identity metadata to any server.
04 Provenance

We preserve where things came from.

STASH is a personal indexer and offline reference tool, not an uncredited rehosting network. Every item saved into your vault permanently preserves its original attribution:

SAVED OBJECT PROVENANCE SCHEMA: V1.0.0
Creator Handle: @samseats
Platform: Instagram Reel
Canonical URL: https://www.instagram.com/reel/C7...
Intended Use: Personal offline indexing & private reference

STASH strips algorithmic ads and platform clutter, but strictly maintains the creator's identity and direct link back to their original work.

05 Access Controls

Your device protects your vault.

STASH provides optional device-level access gating to keep private collections secure:

  • Hardware Biometrics: When enabled, biometric unlock invokes your operating system's native biometric prompt (e.g. Android BiometricPrompt). STASH never accesses, processes, or stores your fingerprint or facial geometry data.
  • Salted PIN Access: Vault PINs are encoded and stored locally on your device with client-side salting (vaultLockService.ts). Your PIN is verified on-device and is never transmitted across the network.
06 Data Portability

Take your library with you. Anytime.

We believe personal knowledge should never be locked behind proprietary silos. STASH provides native, user-controlled export tools built directly into the app (vaultBackupService.ts):

  • Full Vault Archive (.stash): Export your complete collection, folder hierarchy, and checklists as an open, standard JSON archive.
  • Plain-Text Export: Export individual recipes, workouts, or notes directly to standard plain-text formatted for Apple Notes, Google Keep, Notion, or Obsidian.

08 Network Transparency

External technical dependencies.

We believe in total architectural honesty regarding external network requests:

  • Public Media Stream Resolvers: When you submit a link, the app contacts public, stateless media resolver endpoints (such as Cobalt cluster mirrors, TikWM API for TikTok, and public embed endpoints) to resolve public media stream URLs. Only the public URL you submitted is sent.
  • Optional Cloud Sync: If you explicitly choose to sign in with Google (firebaseAuthService.ts), your encrypted vault records synchronize with Google Cloud Firestore (cloudSyncService.ts) to protect your saves across device upgrades. If you do not sign in, this service remains inactive and 100% offline.
  • App Store Distribution: App distribution, updates, and core runtime verification are handled by the Google Play Store and Apple App Store according to their platform terms.
09 User Control

Your data rights & permanent removal.

Because your library is stored locally, you maintain direct control over its retention:

  • Instant Item Deletion: Deleting an item or folder inside STASH immediately removes its record from local storage and deletes associated cached media files.
  • Full Vault Reset: Clearing application data or uninstalling the app permanently removes all local databases, cached clips, and configuration files from your device.
  • Permission Management: You can grant or revoke clipboard access, storage permissions, or notification access at any time through your operating system settings.
