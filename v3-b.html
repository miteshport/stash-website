FAQ

Common questions. Honest answers.

Clear expectations on how STASH works.

What is STASH? +

STASH is your personal library for things worth keeping from around the internet. Share or paste a link from TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, X, Reddit, or web, and STASH extracts available text into structured recipes, workouts, notes, and collections.

Does STASH download video files to my phone? +

No. STASH does not export MP4 files to your device storage or camera roll. STASH saves the structured text, recipe steps, and workout details. Saved video streams play in-app via embedded streaming players.

Does STASH download audio files? +

No. Audio is not downloaded as an MP3 file to your device. Audio associated with saved reels streams in-app with support for background playback and lock-screen controls.

Can I access saved information without internet? +

Yes. Your saved recipes, workouts, notes, and collections stay available on your device offline. Playing the original creator video requires an internet connection.

Do I need an account to use STASH? +

Yes. STASH uses Google Sign-In to secure your library and synchronize your vault across your devices. Your account is private and your library is never made public.

What happens when the source doesn't contain enough information? +

When STASH doesn't know, it doesn't pretend. Missing fields are explicitly marked as "Not provided." We never manufacture numbers, ingredients, or fake instructions. The item remains saved with its source link and creator credit.
